3. How can the functionality and quality of this system be compared to your system?

The reviewed system was built with long prompts with the aid of Cursor agent, similar to mine. I used smaller prompts in comparison, and divided more the content of the prompts. Although not all functionalities were implemented corretly on the reviewed system, Cursor did a great job with the prompts done. I believe that to the reviewed system, as well to my system, in general, Cursor was of great utility. It produced errors to both situations, however, in general, it helped with the creation of the system.
